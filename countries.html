Japan

    Why Visit: A beautiful mix of tradition and technology.

    Highlights: Tokyo, Kyoto temples, cherry blossoms, vending-machine culture.

    Budget Estimate: $1600–$3000 depending on city and season.

    Fun Fact: Japan has more than 5 million vending machines.

Italy

    Why Visit: Art, architecture, and some of the world’s best food.

    Highlights: Rome, Venice canals, Amalfi Coast, Florence museums.

    Budget Estimate: $1500–$2900

    Fun Fact: Italy has the most UNESCO World Heritage Sites in the world.

Peru

    Why Visit: Breathtaking mountains and ancient civilizations.

    Highlights: Machu Picchu, Rainbow Mountain, Cusco, Amazon rainforest.

    Budget Estimate: $1000-$2100

    Fun Fact: Peru is home to over 4,000 varieties of potatoes.
